# U010_Sokar

[English](README.en.md) | [Español](README.md)

## Summary

**SOKAR: La Venganza del Mal** is a complete concept/GDD for a first-person 3D horror game. The player controls **Daniel Hernández**, son of the late Dr. Raúl Hernández, who returns to the family house to collect memories and documents from his father, but ends up trapped in a nightmare shaped by a family curse and by the presence of a demonic entity known as **Sokar**.

The project is designed around exploration, environmental tension, stealth, audio detection, flashlight usage, limited inventory, puzzles, random jumpscares and a psychological horror narrative focused on the mystery of the Hernández family.

## Documented design

- First-person 3D horror.
- Adult target audience.
- Protagonist: Daniel Hernández.
- Antagonist: Sokar, a demonic entity with human and canine traits.
- Main location: the Hernández family house.
- Dual world between everyday reality and a supernatural dimension.
- Oppressive atmosphere based on an abandoned house, basement, shadows, visual distortion and sound.
- Main references: *Madison*, *Visage*, *Outlast* and *P.T.*
- Narrative focused on Dr. Raúl Hernández's death, the father's diary, the family curse and the search for answers.
- Realistic psychological-horror aesthetic with dynamic environment changes.
- Minimalist GUI/HUD to support immersion.
- Initial menu visually integrated into a wall of the house.

## Planned systems

- `FirstPersonController` — first-person movement.
- `InteractionSystem` — contextual interaction with doors, drawers, notes and objects.
- `StealthSystem` — stealth, hiding spots and silent behavior.
- `AudioDetectionSystem` — sound detection during stealth mode.
- `FlashlightSystem` — flashlight, lighting, hidden clues and detection risk.
- `DynamoRechargeSystem` — manual flashlight recharge through a dynamo.
- `InventorySystem` — limited inventory.
- `PuzzleSystem` — puzzles integrated into the environment.
- `JumpscareManager` — fixed and random scares.
- `SokarAI` — chase, stealth mode and threat states.
- `SaveLoadSystem` — new game, load game and progress.
- `UIManager` — minimalist HUD, pause, options and sound feedback.
- `SoundManager` — footsteps, ambience, demon sounds, impacts and tension music.
- `NarrativeManager` — diary, symbols, rituals, dialogues and narrative events.

Finally, it allowed me to practice minimalist UI design focused on immersion and project planning in a team with programming, FX and modeling roles.
